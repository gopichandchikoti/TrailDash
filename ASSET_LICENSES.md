# TrailDash asset provenance

All courier, cargo, gates, garden pylons, modular bridges and skyline geometry is original procedural
geometry authored in `scripts/WorldArt.gd` and `scripts/PlayerController.gd` for this project.
`assets/icon.svg` is original vector artwork. Sound effects are original synthesized waveforms in
`scripts/SoundBank.gd`. No downloaded models, textures, fonts, music or proprietary game assets are used.

Godot Engine is MIT licensed. The packaged Settings → Credits & licenses screen shows the engine's
license, third-party copyright notices, and license texts directly from `Engine.get_license_text()`,
`Engine.get_copyright_info()` and `Engine.get_license_info()`, including its bundled default font.
See https://godotengine.org/license and https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.7.2-stable/COPYRIGHT.txt.

The owner has not selected a public source/art license for TrailDash. No license is granted to the
original game code/assets by the engine's MIT license. Publishing the repository does not change that.
